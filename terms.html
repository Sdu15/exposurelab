Terms of service

What is included at each step, what we do not promise, and how the detailed audit is paid for.

Last updated 6 November 2026 · Exposure Lab, Hong Kong

1. Who these terms are between

These terms cover work provided by Exposure Lab ("we", "us"), a Hong Kong-based funnel diagnosis practice, to the business requesting an audit ("you"). By submitting the free-audit form or booking an audit you accept them.

2. The free 4-point funnel audit

  • Cost: HK$0. No payment details are requested.
  • Scope: a standardised pass across website, offer, paid ads and tracking, plus the basic fixes we would make for anyone with those findings.
  • Delivery: a written reply, normally within 48 hours of a complete request.
  • No obligation: requesting it does not commit you to anything, and does not create a client relationship.

3. The detailed funnel audit — HK$3,000

  • Scope: the same four areas, measured from the accounts and data you give us, with each leak costed per month and the fixes ranked in priority order, delivered as a written report.
  • Payment: HK$1,500 on booking to reserve the analysis slot, and HK$1,500 on delivery.
  • The second payment is due only if you are satisfied with the report. If you tell us it is not useful, you do not pay it and the engagement ends there.
  • Timeline: analysis is delivered within two days of receiving the access and data we request.
  • Your input: we need timely access to the relevant accounts and numbers. Delays in access move the delivery date, not the price.

4. The fix and ads management

Implementation of the recommendations, and any ads management, are separate engagements quoted after the analysis, because scope depends on what it finds. Ads management covers creative and optimisation only. We never hold client media budgets and never take a percentage of ad spend. We do not require a retainer as a condition of the detailed audit.

5. What we do not promise

We do not guarantee revenue, lead volume, ranking or conversion outcomes. Audits are diagnostic: they identify where spend is being lost and what to fix. Any projection we show in a report is an estimate based on the data you supply, and is labelled as such.

6. Your data and your report

The written audit and fix list are yours to keep, share inside your business, and act on — with us or with anyone else. We may describe the type of work we do in general terms, but we will not publish your figures, your name or your brand as a case study without your written permission. Our handling of submitted information is described in the privacy policy.

7. Cancellation

You may cancel the detailed audit before analysis starts and the booking payment is refunded in full. Once analysis has started, the booking payment covers work already done.

8. Liability

Our total liability for any claim connected to an audit is limited to the fees you have paid us for it. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost profit or lost revenue. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited under Hong Kong law.
