Privacy policy

What we collect when you ask for a free funnel audit, why we collect it, and how to get it removed.

Last updated 6 November 2026 · Exposure Lab, Hong Kong

What we collect

When you submit the free-audit form we collect and store only:

  • the email address you enter, and the website or Instagram handle you name;
  • the page you submitted from, the referring page, and any campaign parameters in the link you arrived on (for example utm_source);
  • technical request data that Cloudflare passes to our endpoint: the country of the request, the user-agent string, and a timestamp. We use the transient IP address only to rate-limit abusive submissions; it is not stored with your record.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics on this site. The site sets no cookies of its own.

Why we collect it

  • to prepare and send the audit you asked for, within 48 hours;
  • to reply to you about that request;
  • to understand which pages and campaigns produce genuine enquiries, so we can improve the site.

We do not sell your details, and we do not add you to a bulk marketing list for asking.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare — hosts this site and stores submitted records in Cloudflare KV (including the KV namespace exposurelab-free-audit-leads).
  • Resend — delivers the notification email to us so the request is seen quickly.
  • Airtable — where a submitted request is recorded against our own leads table if the request is relevant to our services.

Each of these is a data processor acting on our instruction; none of them is permitted to use your details for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months so we can follow up if you come back to us, then deleted. If you ask us to delete your record, we action it within 30 days.

Your choices

Email hello@hkexposurelab.com and ask us to access, correct or delete anything we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. We will confirm in writing. You do not have to give a reason.

Security

Requests are transmitted over HTTPS. Secrets and API keys are held as encrypted environment variables in Cloudflare and are never committed to the public source repository. Access to the leads store is limited to Exposure Lab.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Material changes to what we collect will be described here before they take effect.
