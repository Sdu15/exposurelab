FAQ

Digital marketing agency, Hong Kong — common questions.

What do I actually get in the free audit?
A standardised checklist of what your funnel is doing across four areas — website, offer, ads and tracking — plus the basic recommendations we would make to anyone with those findings. It is the same process for every business, which is why it is free. Delivered within two days of your consultation.
If the audit is free, why is the analysis HK$3,000?
Because they are different jobs. The free audit is standardised: it tells you what is typically wrong and what people usually do about it. The analysis is specific: we go into your analytics, your ad accounts and your numbers, measure what each leak is costing you per month, and give you the fixes ranked by what they return. One is a checklist. The other is a diagnosis with your name on it.
Why is there no price for the fix or for ad management?
Because we would be guessing, and you would be paying for our guess. We do not know what your fix costs until we have found the leak and sized it. Once you have the analysis in hand, we quote the work in the open — per project for the build, per month for the ongoing management — and you can decide with the report sitting in front of you.
What if I am not satisfied with the analysis?
The second HK$1,500 is only due on delivery, and only if you are satisfied. If the report does not earn its fee, you do not pay the balance. The risk of a first engagement sits with us, not with you.
Do I have to hire you to do the fix?
No. The report is yours to keep and act on. Implement it yourself, hand it to your team, or take it to another agency. We would rather the analysis be genuinely useful than hold you hostage to it — and a good number of clients do come back once they see how much of it they would rather not do themselves.
How long does each stage take?
The free audit lands within two days of your consultation. The detailed analysis is delivered within a week of booking. The fix runs 3 to 6 weeks depending on scope — and if it finishes early, we will discuss the difference.
What do you need from me to start?
A conversation, and then read access to your analytics and your ad accounts for the paid analysis. If you do not have those set up, that is itself a finding — we will tell you what to put in place before anyone spends another dollar.
You are a two-person studio. Why should I trust you with this?
Because you will be talking to the people doing the work — no account manager, no handoff, no junior exec. We have two clients, not twelve hundred, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. What you get is a written diagnosis of your own numbers, with the fixes ranked by what they return.
Do you work with businesses outside Hong Kong?
Yes. We are based in Hong Kong and quote in HKD, so the engagement works the same way wherever you are. Local market knowledge is our starting point, not our limit.
What will you not do?
We will not promise revenue outcomes, we will not take a cut of your ad budget, we will not hold your media spend, and we will not make outbound AI phone calls. We also will not sell you a twelve-month retainer you cannot leave.
