The short answer

What Exposure Lab does

Exposure Lab is a funnel diagnosis agency in Hong Kong. We audit a business's website, offer, paid ads and tracking to find where marketing spend stops producing customers, then deliver a written report with the exact fixes ranked in priority order.

We publish our prices instead of hiding them: the 4-point funnel audit is free and arrives in writing within 48 hours. The detailed funnel audit is HK$3,000 (~US$380), split HK$1,500 on booking and HK$1,500 on delivery only if you are satisfied. Implementation and ads management are quoted separately, after the analysis.

We do not promise revenue outcomes, we do not take a cut of your media budget, and we do not use automated AI calling. We work with established Hong Kong businesses already spending HK$20,000+ a month on growth.
